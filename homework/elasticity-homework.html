Elasticity Problems

Feb 26: Applications of demand and supply analysis

Homework assigned 2/27, due 3/1.

  1. Cigarettes go from $10 per pack to $20 per pack. The quantity demanded drops from 110,000 packs per week to 90,000 packs per week. What is the price elasticity of demand for cigarettes? Is the demand inelastic, unit elastic, or elastic?

  2. The price of luxury cruises drops from $18,000 per cruise to $14,000 per cruise. The number of passengers rises from 24,000 per year to 40,000 per year. What is the price elasticity of demand for cruises? Is the demand inelastic, unit elastic, or elastic?
